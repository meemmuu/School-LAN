# School LAN: WE School Campus Network

Network design for a school with two buildings 250 m apart (250 students + 20 staff), built in Cisco Packet Tracer.

## What's inside
- `Project.pkt`: the Packet Tracer file
- `configuration.pdf`: device configurations, including the lab login credentials
- `Report.docx`: full report (planning, cabling, budget, schedule)
- `screenshots/`: topology and verification screenshots

## Design
- VLANs: Lab 1 (10), Lab 2 (20), Management (99)
- Multilayer core switches with inter-VLAN routing
- VTP, DHCP pools per VLAN, wireless controller + access points
- Planned: ACLs between VLANs, perimeter firewall, endpoint antivirus

## Physical plan
- Cable lengths per room and per lab
- Patch panel and jack mapping
- 250 m fiber link between buildings

## Budget and schedule
- Total: 958,922 EGP (real Egyptian store prices)
- 5 phases, 1 May to 30 July 2025

## Lab credentials
The device login credentials (VTP and local user) are listed in `configuration.pdf`.
They are for this Packet Tracer lab only and are not used anywhere real.
